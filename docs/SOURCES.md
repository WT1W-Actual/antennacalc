### (b) Italy ruling

`ITALY: DPCM 6 V/m, averaging 1440 min`

- Values, DPCM Allegato B: Table 2 (valori di attenzione) and Table 3 (obiettivi di qualità) both give, for 0.1 MHz < f <= 300 GHz, E = 6 V/m, H = 0.016 A/m, and D = 0.10 W/m² stated for 3 MHz to 300 GHz only. Table 1 (limiti di esposizione) gives 60 V/m and 0.2 A/m for 0.1 to 3 MHz; 20 V/m, 0.05 A/m and 1 W/m² for 3 to 3000 MHz; 40 V/m, 0.01 A/m and 4 W/m² for 3 to 300 GHz.
- Scope: DPCM art. 1, comma 1 covers exposure "generati da sorgenti fisse con frequenza compresa tra 100 kHz e 300 GHz"; comma 4 sends sources "non riconducibili ai sistemi fissi delle telecomunicazioni e radiotelevisivi" to the full 1999/519 restrictions. Legge 36/2001 art. 2, comma 1 covers "gli impianti, i sistemi e le apparecchiature per usi civili ... con frequenze comprese tra 0 Hz e 300 GHz" and "in particolare ... agli impianti radioelettrici". Neither text names amateur stations. ARPAE Emilia-Romagna states that under Legge 36/2001 and the DPCM "sono state 'normate' le emissioni anche per gli impianti radioamatoriali; tale D.P.C.M. ribadisce i limiti di esposizione già a suo tempo stabiliti dal D.M.381/98". On that statement the DPCM applies to amateur stations.
- Averaging: DPCM art. 3, comma 3 and art. 4, comma 1 set "qualsiasi intervallo di sei minuti". D.L. 179/2012 art. 14, comma 8 (converted by Legge 221/2012) keeps six minutes for Table 1 (lettera b) and makes Tables 2 and 3 "media dei valori nell'arco delle 24 ore" (lettere b and c). 24 hours is 1440 minutes.

### (d) CEPT 23 cm ruling

`CEPT 23 cm: 1240-1300`

ECC/DEC/(25)01 does not restrict the amateur service in 1240-1258 MHz. Its decides 2 applies only to administrations "allowing operations of the amateur and amateur-satellite services across their territory in all or part of the frequency band 1258-1300 MHz", and Annex 1 sets e.i.r.p. and transmitter power caps there (for example "1258-1296 MHz: Maximum value of e.i.r.p. = -17 dBW" for narrowband amateur use) rather than removing the band. The amateur allocation stays 1240-1300 MHz.
